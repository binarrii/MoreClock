Before you install
Which Macs does MoreClock support?
MoreClock requires macOS 26 or later. The same download runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
Why might macOS show a warning?
This release is ad-hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple. Review Apple’s instructions for opening an app from an identified source you trust.
Where do I find Settings?
Click a clock in the menu bar and choose Settings. You can also open MoreClock again from Finder or Spotlight, including when all clocks are hidden.
Can I arrange the clocks?
Hold Command and drag a clock to reorder it. Drag it out of the menu bar to hide it; you can show it again in Settings.
Can I automate a clock?
Yes. Shortcuts can add, remove, show and hide clocks, change their time zones and read the time.
