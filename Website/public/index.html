Installation questions

Read the release notes ↗

Which Macs are supported?

macOS 26 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel.

Why might macOS show a warning?

This release is ad-hoc signed and not Apple-notarized. Read Apple’s app-opening guidance.

How do I open clock settings?

Choose Settings from a clock’s menu, or reopen MoreClock from Finder or Spotlight.

Can I arrange the clocks?

Command-drag to reorder. Drag a clock out of the menu bar to hide it.

Does MoreClock support Shortcuts?

Yes. Add, remove, show and hide clocks, change time zones and read the time.
